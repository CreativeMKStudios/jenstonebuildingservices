Privacy

This site is run by Jenstone Industrial Ltd (company number 08057144), trading as Jenstone Building Services. The address is 9 Goldington Road, Bedford, MK40 3JY. Email hello@jenstonebuildingservices.co.uk. Phone 01234 826776.

What we collect

If you call, email or send a WhatsApp message, we receive the details you give us. That is usually your name, phone number, email address, town and a note about the work.

The form on the contact page does not send data to a server we control. It opens an email on your device, addressed to us. Once you send that email, we hold it like any other enquiry.

Why we use it

We use those details to reply, to quote, and to do the work if you hire us. We do not sell your details. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept as business records while they are useful, and for as long as the law asks us to keep records of a job. You can ask us to delete an enquiry that did not become a job.

Cookies and the map

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. The map on the contact page stays unloaded until you press “Show map”. That map is provided by Google, and Google may set its own cookies once it loads.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Write to hello@jenstonebuildingservices.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
