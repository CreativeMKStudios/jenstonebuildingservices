Questions

Before you call

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Bedford and work across Bedfordshire and Buckinghamshire, including Milton Keynes and the villages around Bedford. If you are further out, call and we will say if we can take the job.

Are you an FMB member?

Yes. Jenstone Building Services is listed with the Federation of Master Builders. Members need public liability insurance, a trading history and they sign up to the FMB code of conduct.

Do you work on listed buildings?

Yes. We repair Grade II buildings and other older homes. That includes lime plaster, lime floors, chimney rebuilds and clay tile roofs. Listed building consent is separate from the build, and we work with that.

Do you decorate as well as build?

Yes. Interior and exterior decorating is part of our renovation work, along with kitchens, bathrooms, flooring and joinery. On a full refit, the decorating is planned with the building work.
